Volvo Remote Protocol
=====================

Contributor: ZoeNetwork-
Discord Username: ZoeNetwork-

Protocol Details:
- Vehicle: Volvo (various models)
- Frequency: 433.657070 MHz
- Modulation: OOK ASYNC
- Commands: Unlock (Lu) and Lock (U)

Files:
- Volvo_lu.sub: Unlock command
- Volvo_u.sub: Lock command

Submitted via Automotive Research Firmware (ARF) Discord community
