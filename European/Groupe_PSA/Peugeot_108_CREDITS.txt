Peugeot 108 AM650 Protocol
==========================

Contributor: 𝘼𝙢𝙖𝙪_𝙕𝙚𝙧𝙤
Discord Username: vaidfn

Protocol Details:
- Vehicle: Peugeot 108 (recent model)
- Protocol: AM650
- Frequency: 433.920000 MHz
- Modulation: OOK ASYNC
- Commands: Lock and Unlock
- Repetitions: 5 lock commands, 5 unlock commands

Files:
- AM650_lock_1.sub through AM650_lock_5.sub: Lock commands
- AM650_unlock_1.sub through AM650_unlock_5.sub: Unlock commands

Submitted via Automotive Research Firmware (ARF) Discord community
